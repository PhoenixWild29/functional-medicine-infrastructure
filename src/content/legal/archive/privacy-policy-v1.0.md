# Privacy Policy

_Version 1.0. Effective October 12, 2026._

This Privacy Policy explains how CompoundIQ ("CompoundIQ", "we", "us") collects, uses and shares information through the CompoundIQ website, patient checkout pages and the CompoundIQ platform used by clinics and pharmacies (together, the "Services").

## 1. Who this policy covers and how HIPAA applies

- CompoundIQ provides technology to medical practices ("Clinics") and licensed pharmacies ("Pharmacies"). When we handle a patient's health information on behalf of a Clinic or Pharmacy, we act as their "business associate" under HIPAA. That information is protected health information ("PHI"), and we use and disclose it only as our agreements with those Clinics and Pharmacies and HIPAA allow.
- Your Clinic's and Pharmacy's Notice of Privacy Practices describes how they use your PHI and your HIPAA rights. To access, correct or get an accounting of your PHI, contact your Clinic or Pharmacy; we will help them respond.
- This policy also covers information that is not PHI, such as information about Clinic and Pharmacy staff who use the platform and visitors to our public pages.

## 2. Information we collect

- Patient information provided by your Clinic: name, date of birth, contact details, shipping address, the medications prescribed, the prescriber, the pharmacy, and order status. Clinics enter this information; we do not collect it from you for any other purpose.
- Information you provide at checkout: your confirmation of shipping details and your payment. Card details are entered directly into our payment processor's secure form (Stripe). CompoundIQ does not receive or store your full card number.
- Clinic and Pharmacy user information: name, work email, role, professional identifiers (such as NPI, license numbers and NCPDP ID), business details, and agreements accepted in the platform.
- Technical information: IP address, browser and device type, and log records of sign-ins and actions, used for security, troubleshooting and audit. We record who viewed or changed patient information to protect it.
- We use only cookies needed to keep you signed in and to secure the Services. We do not use advertising, analytics or session-recording tools.

## 3. How we use information

- To provide the Services: create and route prescriptions, collect payments, transmit orders to Pharmacies, track shipments, and send order and payment messages.
- To keep the Services secure and reliable: authenticate users, prevent fraud and misuse, monitor errors, and keep audit logs.
- To meet legal obligations: pharmacy, prescription and payment record-keeping, responding to lawful requests, and enforcing our agreements.
- We do not sell personal information or PHI, and we do not use it for targeted advertising.

## 4. How we share information

- With the Clinic and Pharmacy involved in your order, as needed to prescribe, fill, ship and support it.
- With service providers who help us run the Services, under contracts that limit their use of the information and, where they handle PHI, business associate agreements. These include cloud hosting and database providers, our payment processor, error monitoring, and fax or messaging providers used to deliver orders and notices.
- When required by law, to protect the rights, safety or property of patients, users or CompoundIQ, or in connection with a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, subject to the same protections.

## 5. Do Not Track and other tracking

- We do not track users across other websites over time, and we do not allow third parties to collect information about your online activity across sites through the Services. Because we do not track in this way, we do not change our practices in response to browser "Do Not Track" signals.

## 6. Security

- We protect information with administrative, technical and physical safeguards, including encryption in transit, access controls based on role, two-step sign-in for platform users, audit logging, and storage of credentials in an encrypted vault. No system is perfectly secure, and we will notify affected parties of a breach as HIPAA and other applicable law require.

## 7. Retention

- We keep information for as long as needed to provide the Services and to meet legal and record-keeping requirements, including pharmacy, prescription and payment laws, and then delete or de-identify it. Records that HIPAA requires us to keep are kept for at least six years, and longer where pharmacy or other law requires.

## 8. Your choices and rights

- Patients: for PHI, contact your Clinic or Pharmacy, which controls your records under HIPAA. You may also contact us and we will route your request.
- Platform users: you can review and update your account details in the platform, or ask us to correct them.
- Depending on your state, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information that is not PHI, and to appeal a decision. Send requests through your Clinic, or, if you are a platform user, through your CompoundIQ account contact. We will verify your identity before acting and will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.
- Washington and Nevada residents: we use consumer health data that is not PHI only to provide the Services you requested, we do not sell it, and you may ask us to confirm, access or delete it and to withdraw any consent you gave, using the contact routes in this policy.

## 9. Children

- The Services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information directly from them. A minor's health information may be entered by their Clinic as part of their care and is protected as PHI.

## 10. Changes to this policy

- We will post any changes here with a new effective date. For material changes, we will give notice in the platform or by email before they take effect.

## 11. Contact us

- Patients: contact your Clinic, which will forward privacy requests to us.
- Clinics and Pharmacies: contact your CompoundIQ account contact, Attn: Privacy Officer.
